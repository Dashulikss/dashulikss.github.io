Part 3 — Reading · 5 points

Read the text.

A new routine

Mia lives with her parents and her younger brother in a flat near the town centre. On school days, she gets up at half past seven and walks to school. She usually has lunch at school, but on Fridays she goes home for lunch. After school, she sometimes goes to the library because she likes reading. She doesn't usually go to the shopping centre during the week.

At home, Mia's favourite room is the living room. There is a big sofa next to the window and there are two armchairs opposite the TV. Her brother usually plays computer games in his bedroom. Today, however, Mia isn't reading or watching TV. She is helping her parents clean the flat because some friends are coming in the evening.
26. How does Mia usually get to school?
27. When does Mia go home for lunch?
28. Why does Mia sometimes go to the library?
29. Where is the big sofa?
30. What is Mia doing today?

Part 5 — Writing · teacher assessment

Write 80–100 words.

You have a new English-speaking penfriend. Write an email about your typical day and your home. Include:

  • your usual school/day routine;
  • two free-time activities;
  • where you live and 3–4 things/rooms in your home;
  • what you are doing today.

Эту часть проверяет преподаватель.

Part 6 — Speaking · teacher assessment

Без подготовки:

  1. Tell me about your typical school day.
  2. What do you usually do in your free time?
  3. Describe your home. What rooms and furniture are there?
  4. What are you doing today?

Отметь способность ученицы говорить самостоятельно, использовать Present Simple/Continuous и лексику Units 1–2.

Эту часть оценивает преподаватель во время урока.
